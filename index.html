    The honest answers.

    Real coaches, not bots

    Still unsure? Ask us anything. We usually reply in about 5 minutes.

    Ask on WhatsApp
    I've never worked out. Is this for me?

    Yes. Most of our clients start as complete beginners. Your plan starts exactly where you are.

    Do I need to download an app?

    No. Your plan, videos and check-ins all come through WhatsApp, which you already use.

    Do I need a gym?

    No. We write home, gym or mixed plans around the equipment you have.

    I'm vegetarian. Will the diet work?

    Yes. Meal plans use everyday Indian food, whether you're veg, eggetarian or non-veg.

    Will I actually talk to Biki?

    Elite clients work with Biki one-on-one. Every plan follows his method and is reviewed by his senior team.

    How much time does it take?

    30 to 60 minutes, 3 to 5 days a week. We plan around your calendar.

    Is this another crash diet?

    No. Consistency beats extremes. We build meals and habits you can live with for years, not a 30-day sprint you need a break from.

    Will I need supplements?

    Usually not to start. We fix the fundamentals first: movement, food, sleep, strength and recovery. If your blood work points to something, we'll explain why before suggesting anything.

    How do you decide what's right for me?

    Your own data, not a template. Check-ins, measurements and blood work tell us what's working, and we change the plan when they say so.

    What if it doesn't work for me?

    You're covered by our 14-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked.
